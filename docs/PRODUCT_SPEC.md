# RefiLens — current local research workspace

Corporate Debt & Refinancing Risk Intelligence. A research workspace for examining reported debt, dated maturity disclosures and explicitly hypothetical refinancing sensitivity. It does not assign credit ratings or predict default.

Three traceable calendar FY2025 USD snapshots: Nucor, Steel Dynamics and Reliance. The connected interface provides company selection, financials, source debt maturities, rate/earnings sensitivity, comparison, automated financial review, an annual liquidity model, three editable illustrative cash-stress cases and a printable rule-based brief. The distributor/producer distinction and issuer-specific debt/earnings definitions remain visible.

Financial review reconstructs the original sources and shows debt-note adjustments, cash restrictions and source rounding. Its Excel uses formulas and retains blank personal-review fields. Automated source/arithmetic checks never assert that a human reviewed the figures.

The annual liquidity model uses the next one to five exact source years. Users enter available opening cash, cash floor, operating cash flows, capex, other uses, refinancing share, assumed old/new rates and issuance fees. Results show year-end unfunded cash, a cash bridge, first shortfall year and peak shortfall. Thereafter is excluded rather than assigned to a year. Cash deficits are not filled with an automatic financing plug. All projections and refinancing access remain hypothetical.

Stress scenarios independently evaluate Base/Adverse/Severe through the same cash engine. Each case exposes CFO reduction, capex response, new rate, refinancing share and fees. Results include three cash curves, first/peak shortfall, a reconciled five-driver cash explanation and a two-input cash-flow/rate sensitivity grid. The presets have no calibrated probability or issuer-forecast status. See STRESS_SCENARIOS.md.

The owner-selected option B design separates source references from future hypotheses, prioritizes outcomes, groups the three scenario tools and retains in-session drafts. The selected case transfers exactly into the annual editor, including its assumptions if the horizon is extended. Switching issuer resets drafts. Mobile tables scroll internally and explain that behavior.

Every financial display includes units and an accessible route to filing evidence. Missing values remain unavailable. User assumptions are visually distinct from observations. Users can export scenario inputs/results for reproducibility, and can inspect methodology. No account, paid key or LLM is required for local use.

Acceptance: API/UI serve an authentic financial fact; deterministic tested scenarios respond to user controls; source evidence and limitations are visible; no broken or empty navigation.

## Completed issuer case study

The Nucor FY2025 historical planning case asks whether 2026–2027 maturities, investment and modeled distributions can be funded under weaker operating cash. Eight dated source references distinguish historical observations, management estimates and facility capacity. Four independently calculated annual cases isolate operating pressure, refinancing access/terms and a pause in modeled share repurchases. The conclusion quantifies the remaining chosen-buffer gap and the equal annual cash improvement required to close it mathematically, without assuming that improvement or gap funding is feasible.

The case retains dividends, NCI distributions and share-based compensation tax payments in its response case. The reference for capex comes from the 2026 management estimate; treating it as cash capex and repeating it in 2027 are explicit choices. Source-date boundaries and excluded later principal remain visible. Users can inspect annual bridges, transfer the exact case into the annual editor, download a written memo or export the full evidence/input/output JSON. Other issuers show an honest unavailable state with a link to the Nucor case. No owner authorship/manual-review or current-forecast claim is made.
