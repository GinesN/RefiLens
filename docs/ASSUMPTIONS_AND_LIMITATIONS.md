# Assumptions and limitations

- **Scope:** Five approved USD issuers: NUE, STLD, RS, LECO and ITW. ITW is a consolidated diversified manufacturer, not a pure welding peer. All required fields for the implemented debt, maturity, cash-flow and rate-sensitivity analyses are present; this does not mean every possible financial metric is available. Other tickers are refused until their mapping and compatibility are researched. See COVERAGE_EXPANSION.md for exclusions.
- **Freshness:** A successful live SEC import is an acquisition event. FY2025 statements remain dated 31 December 2025 and were filed in February 2026; they are not November 2026 balances. Quarterly updates and events after the annual filing are outside this view. A new fiscal-year cohort needs explicit review; the importer does not silently substitute an older original filing.
- **Human review:** Zero facts currently claim manual verification. CROSS_CHECKED means agreement between two representations of the same issuer filing, not independent ground truth. A source accounting error can exist in both.
- **Financial precision:** Source disclosures are rounded. Exact Decimal storage avoids additional binary rounding; it does not add source precision. Display abbreviations round; evidence and exports retain base-unit values.
- **Debt scope:** Nucor's company debt includes finance leases and short-term borrowings. Comparison debt subtracts its disclosed USD 258m finance lease liability. STLD/RS use their current/noncurrent long-term debt lines excluding finance leases. Absent borrowing fields are not treated as zero. The debt-note adjustments now reproduce carrying debt at displayed source precision for all five. NUE's rounded principal schedule retains a separate USD 4m difference to note principal, compatible with displayed rounding. No source bucket is changed. See FINANCIAL_REVIEW.md.
- **Cash availability:** Consolidated cash includes joint-venture cash. Cash available to the parent, restricted cash, marketable securities and facility drawability are not modeled. Undrawn facilities are not included in debt or cash.
- **Earnings:** Nucor's analytical EBIT = pretax earnings + expense-positive net interest; it includes nonoperating items. STLD/RS use reported operating income. Each numerator is labelled; the comparison does not rank these different coverage measures. Gross interest is reported expense after the effects of capitalization, not total cash interest paid. Net interest income/expense is never substituted for gross expense.
- **Business comparability:** NUE/STLD produce steel; RS distributes metals. The table compares reported exposures, not equivalent business economics or credit risk. No homogeneous statistical peer group or credit ranking is asserted.
- **EBITDA:** Unavailable without a reviewed reconciliation. No net debt / EBITDA ratio. An optional assumed EBITDA in the Scenario Lab is hypothetical only and must not be repeated as an observed company fact.
- **FCF:** Operating cash flow less cash PP&E purchases; acquisitions excluded. It is not projected cash available to repay debt and is not EBITDA.
- **Scenario timing:** Selected principal is assumed entirely refinanced. Annualized delta uses old/new rate assumptions supplied by the user, not known coupons. It applies once after the selected refinancing, not immediately to all outstanding fixed-rate debt. A broad bucket may be modeled as a whole, never assigned to a specific year. Historical interest is a reference; it may not represent actual interest in a future refinancing year.
- **Annual liquidity model:** A separate hypothetical model rolls year-end cash through entered CFO, capex, other cash uses, contractual maturities, assumed refinancing, fees and cumulative interest changes. It repeats FY2025 flows initially as an editable assumption. Opening cash availability, source short-term repayment timing and refinancing access are assumptions. Finance-lease principal, distributions and acquisitions are excluded unless entered. Negative balances remain unfunded; no automatic facility draw or new loan is inserted. Thereafter stays outside the horizon. See LIQUIDITY_MODEL.md.
- **Stress presets:** Base/Adverse/Severe are illustrative, editable assumption sets, not calibrated business forecasts, recession probabilities or verified financing terms. CFO reductions are flat against FY2025, not compounded. Negative source CFO is made worse using its absolute amount. Capex responses and refinancing choices may offset stress; labels do not guarantee cash ranking. Sensitivity varies only CFO reduction/new rate while holding the selected case’s other inputs fixed. See STRESS_SCENARIOS.md.
- **Nucor case study:** The finished two-year exercise uses the original FY2025 filing published 25 February 2026; it is not a November 2026 assessment or point-in-time trading backtest. It repeats a historical absolute CFO deterioration as a sensitivity, not a probability-calibrated forecast. A management capex estimate becomes an assumed cash budget and is extended one year; payment timing may differ. Historical distributions and withholding repeat only as assumptions. The USD 500m reserve is an analyst choice, not a covenant. Modeled savings/additional cash requirements do not establish feasible actions, financing access or a credit conclusion. See case-studies/NUCOR_FY2025.md.
- **Model boundaries:** No issuer-validated business forecast, financing-access model, covenant analysis, credit rating, default probability, investment recommendation or tax shield. The annual model does not identify intra-year shortfalls, renewed debt maturities or interest costs of uncommitted gap financing. Negative rate spreads are allowed; negative reference interest after adjustment is refused. Zero/nonpositive coverage denominators return unavailable.
- **Software:** Local single-user prototype, loopback servers. Do not expose import/admin capabilities publicly. Optional Docker/PostgreSQL configuration is provided but must be separately integration-tested. No cloud deployment, production authentication, multi-user security, backups or availability guarantee is claimed.
- **Dependencies:** The lockfiles reflect versions available at build time. TypeScript 6 and ESLint 9 are selected because the installed Next ESLint plugins do not support TypeScript 7 / ESLint 10 peer ranges. ESLint 9 carries an upstream deprecation notice; upgrade when compatible plugins are released. No runtime code relies on this development-only package.

The earliest owner task remains independently verifying debt/cash rows, Note 11 and the now-implemented automated principal/carrying reconciliation, then justifying any issuer-specific scenario choices. See OWNER_VERIFICATION_CHECKLIST.md.

- **Added issuer scope:** LECO includes its bank borrowings in 2026 maturities; do not add them again. Its −USD 4,000 source schedule difference is retained at the combined displayed precision bound, not allocated. ITW excludes USD 1.287bn commercial paper from its long-term maturity schedule; default first-year repayment is an editable scenario assumption. Both use reported gross interest and operating income. See FINANCIAL_REVIEW.md.
