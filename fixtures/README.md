## Dated Nucor research evidence

`research/nue-2025-case-evidence.json` and `excerpts/nue-case-evidence.html` are authentic filing derivatives rebuilt from the same SHA-256-checked original filing/companyfacts cache, without network requests. The selected-element wrapper is not the complete source filing. Its own SHA-256 is stored separately from the original whole-response manifest.

The package contains six historical source-reference rows (comparative FY2024/FY2025 CFO, FY2025 common dividends, share repurchases, NCI distributions and share-based compensation tax payments), each accession/period/USD cross-checked. Two additional narrative references are source-extracted: the management capex estimate for 2026 and the reported revolving facility capacity. A forward-looking management estimate is never called a reported realized cash amount. Capacity is never called cash or guaranteed drawability.

This evidence is separate from the 39 persisted observed/control facts. It does not expand issuer coverage, seed projected company facts or imply a human review. The capex budget's conversion into cash assumptions, annual repetitions, CFO stress, future coupons/access/fees, cash floor and response choices exist in the Python case engine/API outputs only. Rebuild with `PYTHONPATH=services/api .venv/bin/python scripts/build_nucor_case_evidence.py` and run the source-boundary/case regression tests before accepting changes.
